Invitation to attend the international scientific conference “Law on corporate social responsibility around the world and the developmental trends of Vietnam in the context of integration”

To honored authors, scientists, researchers, teachers, representatives and guests

On the 6th of November, 2021, Binh Duong University, with the cooperation of Belarus State University, will be holding an international scientific conference: “The law on corporate social responsibility around the world and the developmental trend of Vietnam in the context of integration”. The conference will be held virtually by Zoom meetings.

Binh Duong University cordially invites interested authors, scientists, researchers, teachers, representatives and guests to attend and present at the conference.

For the success of the conference, the Organizing committee would like to ask you to register your attendance before the 20th of October, 2021, through the scanning of the QR bar code (attached to this letter).

Binh Duong University is honored to welcome your attendance.

Sincerely./.
