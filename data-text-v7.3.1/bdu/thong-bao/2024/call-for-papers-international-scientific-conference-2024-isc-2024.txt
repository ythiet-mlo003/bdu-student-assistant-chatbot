CALL FOR PAPERS
INTERNATIONAL SCIENTIFIC CONFERENCE
2024 - ISC 2024

“GREEN VALUES FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT”

Dear researchers and colleagues,

In the context of increasing impacts of climate change, green growth
is considered to be an effective method to develop for many countries.
Based on science and technology, the application of digital technology
and digital transformation, the growth model which includes reducing the
intensity of greenhouse gas emissions through the economical and efficient
exploitation and use of energy and resources will help us develop green,
sustainable infrastructure and build a green lifestyle, ensuring an effective
green transition in a variety of fields.

With the global trend and concern, we are pleased to annouce the
Call for Papers for the International Scientific Conference 2024 (ISC 2024) on
“Green values for sustainable development”. The conference will be held
by Binh Duong University on September 21, 2024.

We cordially invite you, the domestic and foreign scientists, experts,
lecturers, researchers, postgraduates, students and learners domestically
and internationally in related fields, to write and submit papers on the
topics of the conference. The papers will be peer-reviewed and edited for
publication in the Conference Proceedings (with ISBN index) with presentation
opportunities at the conference. The topics of focus on the Green values for
sustainable development in specific fields (but not limited to):

[NỘI DUNG ẢNH TRONG BÀI — OCR]

Economics, Finance & Business Administration;

Biotechnology, Food Science & Pharmacy.
