CALL FOR PAPERS
Dear Colleagues,
We are pleased to announce the Call for papers for the International Youth Conference (IYC) - 2022
with the theme “Youth responsibility in the context of the Covid-19 Pandemic”. The conference will be organized
by Binh Duong University (BDU) on April 24th, 2022.
The IYC aims to provide global youth with an opportunity to share and discuss ideas so that youth are
able to promote their community responsibilities during the Covid-19 pandemic. The target audience for the
IYC is primarily youth all over the world, including students from BDU’s partners. In addition, all students at
BDU are welcomed to attend IYC events which are only open to registered delegates.
The Conference is to be held online through Zoom and will feature keynote and plenary parallel sessions
with distinguished speakers. The language used in the conference is English.
You are invited to submit papers with the topics including, but not limited to:
1.Youth’s responsibility for the health of themselves, their families, society (community) and the natural
environment (including treatment of domestic waste, medical waste; reducing energy consumption,
greenhouse gases;...) in the context of the Covid-19 pandemic.
2. Youth’s active role in research and study during the Covid-19 pandemic.
3. Capacity building and skill development for effective learning in the context of the Covid-19 pandemic.
