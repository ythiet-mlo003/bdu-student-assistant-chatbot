OPENING LETTER
INVITATION TO THE INTERNATIONAL YOUTH CONFERENCE 2022
“YOUTH RESPONSIBILITY IN THE CONTEXT OF
THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC”
Dear Colleagues,
In the complicated times of the Covid-19 pandemic, apart from the practical implementation
of prevention and control measures, undoubtedly the awareness and responsibility of each
global individual play an important role in jointly tackling the challenges of Covid-19. Certainly,
having an indispensable role in the prevention of the disease, the youth are key components,
as well as the main resource in the modern era.
With the desire to build a global sharing space to help the youth take on their responsibilities
in the fight against the coronavirus, Binh Duong University is organizing The International
Youth Conference (IYC) - 2022.
The conference aims to exchange and discuss “Youth responsibility in the context of
Covid-19 Pandemic” with the following topics:
1. Youth’s responsibility for the health of themselves, their families, society (community)
and the natural environment (including treatment of domestic waste, medical waste;
reducing energy consumption, greenhouse gases; ...) in the context of the Covid-19
pandemic.
2. Youth’s active role in research and study during the Covid-19 pandemic.
3. Capacity building and skill development for effective learning in the context of the
Covid-19 pandemic.
And other related topics.

Estimated time & date of the conference: 13:00 - GMT +7, April 24, 2022 (Sunday);
Location: Binh Duong University, 504 Binh Duong Avenue, Hiep Thanh Ward, Thu
Dau Mot City, Binh Duong Province, Vietnam;
Conference format: Online via Zoom;
Language used in the conference: English.
