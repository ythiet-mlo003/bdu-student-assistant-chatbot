NATIVE ENGLISH TEACHER RECRUITMENT

Thứ năm - 31/01/2019 21:46

Our English center is an accredited English center under the Open Training - Research -Development Institute. Established in 2008, we provide Basic and Intensive English Program to our partners’ activities, language centers, public schools and enterprises.

The English center works with clients to design courses to meet the specific needs of industries and individuals.We have assisted thousands of students in their pursuit of the English language proficiency needed to reach their educational and career goals.

We are continuously looking for full time and hourly paid teachers to work at our English center in Thu Dau Mot Town, Binh Duong Province from Jan 2019

The successful applicant will be a Native English speaker or European with native accent with:

B.Ed / PGCE in TESOL / TEFL / Applied Linguistics or

Bachelor’s Degree (or equivalent) with English language teaching

Age: 21~55

Photos, Visa

All salaries are based on qualifications, experience and your teaching performance. The salary is from 1,800-2,500 USD/ month (80 hours).

The working time normally is as follows:

Mon-Sun morning : From 7 am - 11 am

Mon-Sat afternoon: From 1 pm - 5 pm

Mon-Sat evening : From 5.30 pm - 9 pm

NOTE:

Detailed lesson plans and flash cards are provided in assigned projects.

Communication support for the events like marketing campaign…

TEACHING BENEFITS:

Work permit and Accommodation allowance

A one-way ticket after one -year contract.

Round ticket aftertwo- year contract.
